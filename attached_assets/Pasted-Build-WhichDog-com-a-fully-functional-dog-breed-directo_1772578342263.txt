Build WhichDog.com — a fully functional dog breed directory for all 205 AKC-recognized breeds.

The site's purpose is to educate people on dog breeds and help them find the right dog for their lifestyle. The tone should be warm, approachable, and educational — not clinical. Think "knowledgeable friend who loves dogs" rather than encyclopedia. Design-wise, model the layout after WhichLeader.com: clean card grids, two-column detail pages, prominent search/filter bar, responsive design, dark mode support.

Data Model
Each breed entry needs the following fields:

Identity:

Breed name (e.g., "Golden Retriever")
Slug (URL-friendly, e.g., "golden-retriever")
AKC breed group (Sporting, Hound, Working, Terrier, Toy, Non-Sporting, Herding)
Origin country
Year AKC recognized
Image URL (placeholder initially, admin-editable)
Physical Characteristics:

Size category (Small, Medium, Large, Giant)
Weight range (e.g., "55–75 lbs")
Height range (e.g., "21–24 inches")
Lifespan (e.g., "10–12 years")
Coat type (Short, Medium, Long, Wiry, Curly, Double, Hairless)
Coat colors (text, e.g., "Golden, Light Golden, Dark Golden")
Shedding level (Low, Moderate, High, Very High)
Hypoallergenic (boolean)
Temperament & Behavior:

Temperament summary (2–3 sentence description)
Energy level (Low, Moderate, High, Very High)
Trainability (Easy, Moderate, Challenging, Independent)
Barking tendency (Rare, Occasional, Frequent, Vocal)
Good with kids (1–5 rating)
Good with other dogs (1–5 rating)
Good with cats (1–5 rating)
Stranger friendliness (1–5 rating)
Apartment suitable (boolean)
Care Requirements:

Exercise needs (minutes per day, e.g., "60–90 min")
Grooming frequency (e.g., "Weekly brushing, professional grooming every 6–8 weeks")
Common health issues (text list, e.g., "Hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, cataracts")
Diet notes (text, e.g., "Prone to obesity — measure portions carefully")
Training notes (text, specific to breed)
Content Sections (longer text):

Breed history/origin story (2–3 paragraphs)
Personality deep dive (2–3 paragraphs)
Living with this breed (what daily life looks like)
Health and care guide
Fun fact (one-liner)
Famous quote or saying about the breed
Pages
Homepage
Hero section with tagline: "Find your perfect companion" and a prominent "Take the Quiz" call-to-action button
Search bar that filters breeds by name in real-time
Filter controls: breed group, size, energy level, good with kids, apartment suitable, hypoallergenic
Sort options: alphabetical, popularity, size, lifespan
Grid of breed cards showing: breed image/placeholder, breed name, breed group badge, size, energy level, a 1-line temperament summary
Each card links to the breed's detail page
Stats bar showing: "205 AKC-Recognized Breeds | 7 Breed Groups"
Individual Breed Page (/breed/[slug]/)
Breadcrumb: Home > [Breed Group] > [Breed Name]
Header with breed name, breed group badge, origin country flag
Two-column layout:
Left column: "About [Breed Name]" with personality deep dive, then "Living With a [Breed Name]"
Right column: Quick Facts card (size, weight, height, lifespan, energy, trainability, shedding, hypoallergenic), then Compatibility Ratings card (kids, dogs, cats, strangers, apartment — shown as visual rating bars)
Full-width sections below the two columns:
"History & Origins" section
"Health & Care" section
"Recommended Products" section (food, toys, grooming tools — with space for sponsor placements, each recommendation includes product name, why it's good for this breed, and link placeholder)
"Finding a [Breed Name]" section — breed-specific sourcing tips plus link to the main ethical sourcing guide
FAQ section for SEO (3–5 common questions about the breed, answered concisely)
"Back to all breeds" navigation link (or "Back to quiz results" if they came from the quiz)
"Which Dog Is Best for You?" Quiz (/quiz/)
Prominent, visually engaging page — this is a primary feature, not an afterthought
8–10 questions, one per screen, with progress bar:
Where do you live? (Apartment, House with small yard, House with large yard, Rural/farm property)
How much time can you spend exercising your dog each day? (Under 30 min, 30–60 min, 60–90 min, 90+ min)
How much experience do you have with dogs? (First-time owner, Some experience, Experienced owner, Professional/trainer)
Do you have children? (No, Yes — under 5, Yes — 5 to 12, Yes — teenagers)
Do you have other pets? (No, Yes — dogs, Yes — cats, Yes — both)
How much grooming are you willing to do? (Minimal, Weekly brushing, Regular grooming, Doesn't matter)
How important is a hypoallergenic breed? (Not important, Somewhat important, Very important)
What size dog do you prefer? (Small, Medium, Large, No preference)
What personality do you value most? (Loyal and protective, Playful and energetic, Calm and gentle, Independent and low-maintenance)
How much training time can you commit? (Minimal — want an easy dog, Moderate — happy to do basic training, Extensive — enjoy the challenge, Love training — want a working partner)
Results page: Top 5 recommended breeds with match percentage, brief explanation of why each breed matches, link to full breed page
"Retake Quiz" and "Share Results" options
Store quiz results in session so "Back to quiz results" works from breed pages
Ethical Sourcing Guide (/find-a-dog/)
Take a clear, opinionated stance: never buy from pet stores or puppy mills
Sections:
"Why Where You Get Your Dog Matters" — brief, impactful intro about puppy mills
"How to Find a Responsible Breeder" — AKC Marketplace, breed club referrals, questions to ask breeders, red flags to watch for, what a good breeder looks like (health testing, socialization, contracts)
"Adopting From a Rescue or Shelter" — breed-specific rescues, general shelters, what to expect, benefits of adopting adult dogs
"Red Flags to Avoid" — pet stores, online sellers without verification, breeders who won't let you visit, no health clearances, multiple breeds available
"Resources" — links to AKC Marketplace, Petfinder, Adopt-a-Pet, breed-specific rescue organizations
This page should be linked from every breed page
Product Recommendations (/products/)
Overview page organized by category: Food, Toys, Training, Grooming, Health/Supplements
Each product listing: product name, photo placeholder, description, which breeds/sizes it's best for, why it's recommended (health-focused — emphasize nutrition, natural ingredients, durability)
Filter by breed size (Small, Medium, Large) and category
Individual breed pages pull relevant products into their "Recommended Products" section
Design the layout with future sponsor placements in mind — each product card should have space for a "Sponsored" badge and affiliate link
Strong editorial stance: recommend healthy, high-quality products. No junk food, no cheaply made toys. Frame recommendations around dog health and longevity.
HTML Sitemap (/sitemap/)
All breeds organized by breed group
Links to quiz, ethical sourcing guide, product recommendations
Admin Panel (/admin/)
Password-protected (use ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variable)
List all 205 breeds with search
Edit any breed's fields including all text content, ratings, and image URL
Add/edit/remove product recommendations
Preview changes before saving
Technical Requirements
Full SEO: unique title tags, meta descriptions, Open Graph tags for every page
XML sitemap at /sitemap.xml with all breed pages, quiz, products, and guide pages
robots.txt allowing all pages except /admin
Responsive design: mobile-first, works beautifully on phone, tablet, and desktop
Dark mode toggle
Fast loading: lazy load images, skeleton loading states
Structured data (JSON-LD) for each breed page — use the "Animal" or "Thing" schema type
Canonical URLs with trailing slashes
data-testid attributes on all interactive and meaningful display elements
Data Seeding
Pre-populate all 205 AKC-recognized breeds with accurate data for every field
Breed groups: Sporting (32 breeds), Hound (34), Working (31), Terrier (31), Toy (25), Non-Sporting (29), Herding (23)
Use real, accurate data for all physical characteristics, temperament ratings, and text content
Product recommendations: seed 20–30 high-quality product recommendations across all categories, mapped to appropriate breed sizes
All data should be editable via admin panel and synced on startup via upsert (same pattern as WhichLeader.com)
Design Notes
Color-code breed groups like WhichLeader color-codes continents (each group gets a unique color for badges and filters)
Breed cards on homepage: show image placeholder (initials-based like WhichLeader's LeaderAvatar until real photos are added), breed name, group badge, and 2–3 key traits as small pills (e.g., "High Energy", "Good with Kids", "Large")
Compatibility ratings on breed pages: use filled/empty paw icons instead of stars
Quiz: use illustrations or icons for each answer option, make it feel fun and engaging, not like a form
That covers the full scope. You can copy this entire prompt into a new Replit project to get started. A few notes:

Start with the data model and seeding — getting all 205 breeds populated accurately is the biggest chunk of work.
The quiz logic is the feature that will set this apart from other breed directories. It's worth getting the matching algorithm right.
Product recommendations are designed to be sponsor-ready from day one, even if you launch without sponsors initially.
The ethical sourcing page is your credibility differentiator — most breed sites don't take a clear stance.
